# ST04 Sipje

## One Line

Sipje images are about rites for the dead, ghostly service, grave gates, funeral
objects, and the pressure of unseen voices.

## Direction

- Use white rice, black soil, funeral cloth, bells, mist, and grave doors.
- Make ghosts feel embedded in ritual, not like generic monsters.
- Keep death ceremonial and social, not just frightening.

## Avoid

- haunted-house cliches
- gore-first horror
- clean necromancer fantasy
