# ST03 Saro

## One Line

Saro images are about masks, bells, tricks, inverted rules, and laughter that is
almost but not quite safe.

## Direction

- Keep folk-play rhythm in the uncanny.
- Masks and bells should feel like rules of the world, not props.
- Humor and fear should appear in the same shape.

## Avoid

- slasher horror masks
- western demon faces
- polished trickster fantasy
