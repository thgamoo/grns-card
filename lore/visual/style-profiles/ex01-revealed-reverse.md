# EX01 Revealed Reverse

## One Line

EX01 images should feel like an observer's report finding the ugly underside of
war: requisition, flight, spies, siege, and contamination.

## Direction

- Darker and more political than base-set cards.
- Use witness distance, scraps of evidence, burned borders, and suspicious
  ritual traces.
- Let faction style profiles remain visible, but cover them with uncertainty.

## Avoid

- clean heroic expansion art
- purely monstrous spectacle
- modern documentary realism
