# ST01 Yemaek

## One Line

Yemaek images are about northern force, red-eyed possession, rough armor, and
the will to break gates.

## Direction

- Use hard silhouettes, iron, horse tack, blood-dark red, and cold northern air.
- Violence should feel ritualized and possessed, not cinematic.
- Gates, orders, banners, and military rows are key shapes.

## Avoid

- generic barbarian fantasy
- realistic war-movie dust
- polished medieval knight armor
