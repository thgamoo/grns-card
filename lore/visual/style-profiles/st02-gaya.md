# ST02 Gaya

## One Line

Gaya images are about furnaces, revolt, cast iron, gold authority, and bodies
reshaped by workshops and conspiracy.

## Direction

- Use furnace light, coal ash, rough cast iron, molds, chains, and gold crowns.
- Metal should look handmade, heavy, and politically charged.
- Rebellion should be visible through material culture, not modern protest
  imagery.

## Avoid

- clean steampunk machinery
- science-fiction robots
- smooth fantasy golems
