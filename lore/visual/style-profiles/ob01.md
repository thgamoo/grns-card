# OB01 Onboarding

## One Line

First-gate onboarding images should feel approachable and readable, but still
slightly ritual, strange, and unsafe.

## Direction

- Small animal bodies can be charming, but they should not become mascot art.
- Gate, burial, sacrifice, and lord-reveal rules should leave visible world
  pressure in the image.
- Use old paper warmth, muted ritual red, rainy blue gray, gate wood, and ink
  black.
- Keep compositions clear enough to teach a card, but not UI-like.

## Avoid

- children's picture-book softness
- realistic wildlife rendering
- bright toy colors
- modern tutorial icon language
